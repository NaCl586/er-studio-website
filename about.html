About ER Studio

Who Are We?

ER Studio is a game development team with a focus on creating educational games, while creating for-fun games on the side as well.

The games we create are made to order according to our clients' needs. We've developed games about a variety of subjects, such as kindergarten education, high school chemistry, and high school mathematics , each built from ground up according to our clients' request and application.

Our Vision

To create engaging games that players lose themselves in the fun and make learning enjoyable, motivating players to explore and grow.

Our Mission

To collaborate with developers, educators, and players in crafting innovative, interactive, and high-quality games that inspire growth, connection, and lasting enjoyment.
