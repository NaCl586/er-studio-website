Digestive
Inside Out

This 2D isometric management simulation game casts players as a body operator manager or inspector tasked with overseeing the human digestive system across six strategic hubs using memory-based mini-games.

Players must carefully coordinate everything from mechanical food processing in the Mouth to enzymatic chemical reactions in the Stomach and Small Intestine, ensuring that nutrients are perfectly absorbed and metabolic waste is properly eliminated.
