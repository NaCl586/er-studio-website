Ninja Math Quest
Master of the Universe

A narrative-driven game based on the permutations, combinations, and probability subjects of 12th-grade mathematics.

In this game, players follow a ninja on his journey to become the master of the universe, encountering many math-based obstacles along the way. The gameplay involves point-and-click object interactions that help players visualize and grasp the physicality of various math cases, before prompting them for a proper, final answer.
