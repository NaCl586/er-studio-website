Equilibrium Shift

An educational puzzle game based on the chemical equilibrium topic in 11th-grade chemistry, where players must manipulate reaction conditions to achieve specific equilibrium objectives.

In this game, players solve chemistry puzzles by controlling factors that affect equilibrium. Through hands-on experimentation, they develop an intuitive understanding of chemical equilibrium and Le Chatelier's Principle.
